Conference: Supercomputing 2025 in St. Louis

Published

September 5, 2025

I am excited to share that I will be attending Supercomputing 2025 (SC25) this November in St. Louis, Missouri. SC is the leading international conference for high performance computing, networking, storage, and analysis. I am particularly interested in how large-scale computation enables generative Bayesian modeling, causal inference, and machine learning for environmental data. I’m looking forward to the tutorials, talks, and workshops!
