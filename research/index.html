Research

My research program combines ecological theory, statistics, and high-performance computing to explain how climate variability and environmental uncertainty shape biological timing and performance.

I focus on phenology as a process linking individual physiology to fitness, population dynamics, and community assembly. Most of my empirical work is in alpine tundra and agricultural systems, where shifts in timing affect ecosystem function, crop yield, and climate adaptation.
